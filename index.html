More about me

I believe that software is meant for humans — building products that are intuitive and easy to use has always been my priority. Even as a backend engineer, I think about how the frontend will consume my data, how fast my code executes, how secure user data remains, and how quickly results reach the user.

I also believe good software is maintainable software: clean architecture and well-structured code reduce long-term costs and enable faster iteration when business needs change. With over 4 years of experience across OTT streaming, e-commerce, and logistics, I bring a user-first mindset to building scalable, reliable backend systems.
